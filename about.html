About Me

Bio

An Information Systems student who finds beauty in calm moments and gentle visuals. I love creating infographics and designs that feel soft, meaningful, and comforting. For me, design is a quiet way to express thoughts that words sometimes can’t — a balance between structure and imagination.

Education

Currently studying Information Systems at ITPLN, where I learn how technology, data, and creativity can come together to tell stories in a visual form.

My academic journey has helped me understand both the logic behind systems and the art of presenting ideas beautifully and clearly.

Personal Notes

Outside of school and design, I enjoy slow mornings, cozy movies, and exploring little things that make life feel softer.

This space is my gentle corner — a place where I share what I create, what inspires me, and pieces of the world that make me smile.
